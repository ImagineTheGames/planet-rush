Planet Rush — Concept Round 2

Shortlist variants per your notes: Paper Cutout with darker space and whiter stars, pushed across three levels of exaggeration · Soft Flat on deep space · Salvage-Punk de-oranged for vacuum. All scenes now use the fog HUD: only your planet's HP (top-right + a bar over the planet), enemy HP hidden until scouted, minimap in every scene.

Exaggeration: maximum — scalloped pop-up planet, ships hanging from visible threads, layered wavy backdrop strips, huge stage-light shadows, ticket-stub HUD. Most theatrical; the planet-death beat (a paper world slumping on its string) would be the best in any theme. Risk: heaviest shadows and layers, strings must not read as gameplay elements; likely too precious for a fast PvP brawl at full intensity — but its death moment could be borrowed by F1/F2.

What changed: all rust/amber/torch-orange replaced — hulls are gunmetal, corrosion is teal patina (oxidized copper reads "old machine in vacuum," not "cave"), ore and hazard stripes are signal yellow, and the mining beam is a cold plasma-blue cutting torch. Keeps the junkyard wit, loses the cavern. Risk: mud problem solved by yellow/blue signals, but the palette is somber — cheek now lives entirely in shapes (rivets, duct-tape decals, scaffolds).

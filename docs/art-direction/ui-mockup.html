PLANET RUSH — the whole screen

Everything locked so far, composed: Cold Vacuum palette · earthlike planets (random P5–P8 per player) · redone cannons · ship classes with player colors · fog HUD (own HP only, scouted enemy rings) · Audiowide as the game's display font throughout (self-hosted in production; this page pulls it from Google Fonts, so it needs internet once to render). 16:9, in-match, mid-game.

TOP-LEFT — Ore at a glance: filled squares = your hold (squares grow with cargo upgrades, flash when full); ORE = banked total. No words to parse mid-fight.
TOP-CENTER — ASTEROID WAVE clock: wave 3 of 5, next drop in 0:47, match time 07:12. Named in full so players know exactly what's coming.
TOP-RIGHT — MY PLANET only, in your player color. No other HP bars anywhere on the HUD — fog rule.
CENTER — Build & Upgrade wheel (open near your planet): every segment is labeled with its target — TURRET 3 · SHIELD 5 · REPAIR CORE 1 · UPGRADE SHIP ▸ · BANK. Bare numbers are costs, nothing else; rates and effects are learned by doing. Four segments act on your planet, one on your ship — the labels say which, so nobody has to guess. Live ore in the hub.
PLANETS — Beacon ring = owner color, always visible (identity). Damage ring = HP, only within sensor range: Vulture (red, top-right) is scouted at 55%; Warden (violet, top-left) shows "??" — out of range.
SHIPS — Narrow hull bars float directly over every ship in its owner's color. Full class stats and upgrade tiers appear only inside the upgrade menu — not on the HUD.
BOTTOM STRIP — Controls always readable: keys in yellow, actions in grey. Renders the active device's bindings (keyboard/mouse here; gamepad shows LS·RS·RT·Y·LT·D-PAD, touch shows gesture hints) and swaps automatically.
BOTTOM-RIGHT — Minimap: 8 planet dots in player colors (you outlined white), center asteroid field, X = dead planet. Last-known positions only.
WORLD — Your P5 planet with hazard-ring shield + T5/T6 cannons; enemy Interceptor (red #2) mining the contested field; Bolt's wreck bottom-right, ore glinting.

Upgrade panel — the only place stats live

Second screen of the wheel: E opens Build & Upgrade → the cyan UPGRADE SHIP ▸ segment opens this. The arrow marks it as the only segment that leads somewhere rather than spending immediately. Stats are hidden during normal play and revealed here, where they're a decision instead of clutter — current value → next tier, with the ore cost.

WHY HERE — During play the HUD shows only what you act on: ore, wave clock, your planet, hull bars. Stats are a shopping decision, so they live in the shop.
CLASS-AWARE — Bars show your class base (Excavator's beam starts high, engine low), so the panel teaches your hull's identity while you spend.
ONE CURRENCY — Same ORE total as the build menu: every tier bought here is a turret not built. The triangle, priced.

The bench (post-week-one hulls)

Wedge (S2), Manta (S5), and Prospector (S6) stay in the concept library as future classes — a glass-cannon duelist, a stealth-leaning skirmisher, and a support/repair hull are natural slots. Not scheduled, not balanced, not in week one.

Why this quartet works

It's a rock-paper-scissors around the triangle: the Interceptor punishes greedy miners but loses to defended planets · the Excavator out-economies everyone but is prey in the open · the Hauler cracks sieges and hauls safely but can't chase · the Vanguard never gets countered and never counters. Bot personalities map onto hulls (Bolt/Sable → Interceptor, Foreman/Warden → Excavator, Rusty/Patch → Hauler, Vulture → Vanguard), so reading a silhouette tells you who you're dealing with — silhouette IS information, which is why the hull stays steel and only the trim takes player color.

Planet Rush · Ship class design · QA guardrail: no class exceeds 55% win rate in mirror-vs-field harness runs. Class picks happen in the lobby (day 4 milestone); the Vanguard is the pre-selected default so onboarding never blocks on the choice.

PLANET RUSH — SCENE GALLERY

Every distinct moment the ruleset can produce, drawn in the frozen Cold Vacuum style. If a scene here isn't fun to look at or isn't legible at a glance, that's a design bug found before day 1 — which is the point of drawing them.

The Triangle — Mine

Mining run

Beam on rock, cracks spreading, freed ore drifting into the hold. Hold squares fill one by one — the greed clock starts here.

Asteroid wave drop

A new wave spawns nearer the center and the whole lobby converges on the same rock. The metronome that forces contact.

The Triangle — Attack

Sieging a defended planet — alone

Two turrets + shield vs one ship. Your hull is bleeding, their core is untouched. The GDD's promise: an occupied planet punishes a lone attacker.

Two attackers crack the turtle

Unspoken alliance: constant damage means shields never regen and the owner can't channel repair. Why being the leader is dangerous.

The Triangle — Defend

Home defense — the ship is the wall

Turrets deter; you and the turrets together kill. The raider breaks off burning. Being home is what makes turrets matter.

Repair channel

Parked at your own core, ore draining, HP crawling back. Any hit cancels it. Repair costs the two things you can't spare: ore and time.

Ship-to-ship

Dogfight in open space

Class identity decides it: the Excavator wins if it lands the beam, the Interceptor wins if it doesn't have to. Same beam that mines.

Interception — the loaded hauler

A full hold is a target. Kill it and half that ore spills into space. The reason "one more rock" is a real gamble.

Consequences

The alarm — the game's whole design in one frame

Deep in the field, hold nearly full, and your core starts taking hits. Every system exists to make this second hurt.

Death & spill

Ships are cheap: 5 seconds, upgrades intact. But half your hold is now loose in space for anyone. The cost is time and position, never progress.

Endgame

Wreck scavenging

The dead player's ore stays on the map, and nobody can haul it away in one trip. Small cargo turns a corpse into a battlefield.

Collapse phase

Wave 5 mined out. Shields dead, repair off, wrecks everywhere, two cripples circling. The rule that guarantees the match ends.

Bookends

Match start

Spawn protection glowing, 3 ore in the bank, no defenses anywhere. The opening choice: turret, upgrade, or bank it?

Victory / defeat

Same screen either way, with REMATCH as the loud button. Eliminated players see this instantly — nobody watches bots finish.

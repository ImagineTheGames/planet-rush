Asteroids — the exploration you were owed

The economy object: it must read "shootable rock," crack visibly in three stages, and its ore payout must be legible before you commit beam time. Six options.

A1 — Shard

Hard angular chunk, yellow ore veins along fracture lines; mining widens the veins until it splits. Cleanest read.

A2 — Ice-Veined Boulder

Rounded body, glowing frost veins that brighten as it nears breaking, embedded nuggets. Prettiest; softest read.

A3 — Rubble Cluster

Parent rock + satellite pebbles: pebbles pop first for fast small payouts, then the core. Best moment-to-moment feedback.

A4 — Hollow Husk

Half-mined already: a yellow-rimmed cavity with one fat nugget. Storytelling rock — someone was here before you.

A5 — Geode Cluster

Dark rock studded with exposed ore crystals — the payout is countable before you shoot (three crystals = three ore). Most strategic read.

A6 — Patina Drifter

Weathered rock with teal oxidation bands matching the planets — the "old solar system" look. Ties world and economy together visually.

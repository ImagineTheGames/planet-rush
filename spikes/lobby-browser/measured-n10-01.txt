
── §1  ONE ROW — what a browse row carries ─────────────────────────────────

one row, verbatim off the wire:
    {"id":"w5NFiVlBt8EGENJM","owner":"P9EW46","region":"iad","size":8,"mode":"ffa","players":1,"joinableSeats":7}

fields: id, owner, region, size, mode, players, joinableSeats
a room code anywhere in the payload? no
one row weighs 109 B of JSON

── §2  ONE LISTING — the deployed fleet at its ceiling, on the wire ────────

fleet:            2 Machines × 6 rooms = 12 listed
JSON body:        1364 B
whole response:   1511 B  (status line + headers + body)
the request:      232 B  (a bare CORS-simple GET, no preflight)
round trip:       1743 B

per room:         114 B of body
empty fleet:      the same route on an empty registry —
                  411 B round trip, body {"rooms":[],"asOf":1700000000000}

── §3  ONE BROWSER — what an idle browsing player costs, per minute ────────

poll cadence:     5 s (D4; the list cannot be fresher than the
                  5 s heartbeat that feeds it, so a faster poll buys nothing)

  ┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
  │ ONE PLAYER, BROWSE ON SCREEN, PER MINUTE                   │
  │    requests:  12                                           │
  │    bytes:     20916  (20.4 kB, full fleet, uncompressed)   │
  │ ONE PLAYER, TAB HIDDEN OR OFF THE BROWSE SEGMENT           │
  │    requests:  0        bytes:  0                           │
  └────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

   2 s poll → 30 req/min,  52290 B/min, 2.99 MB/hour
   5 s poll → 12 req/min,  20916 B/min, 1.20 MB/hour
  10 s poll →  6 req/min,  10458 B/min, 0.60 MB/hour

── §4  A CLASSROOM — thirty browsers, and the CPU under them ───────────────

    1 browsing players →   0.2 req/s, 1.2 MB/hour at the allocator
    6 browsing players →   1.2 req/s, 7.2 MB/hour at the allocator
   30 browsing players →   6.0 req/s, 35.9 MB/hour at the allocator
  100 browsing players →  20.0 req/s, 119.7 MB/hour at the allocator

   2 Machines × 6 rooms =  12 rooms → 60.4 µs to build one listing
  20 Machines × 6 rooms = 120 rooms → 487.5 µs to build one listing

  (the listing derives two HMACs per room; that is what the µs above includes)

── §5  THE ALTERNATIVE — the fan-out this route was chosen over ────────────

GET /rooms/:code — one room's advert: 496 B round trip (status 200)
reading the fleet a row at a time: 12 requests per refresh instead of 1,
i.e. 144 allocator requests/min per open browser at a 5 s poll.

── FOR D5 — the one number, and what it does NOT say ───────────────────────

AN IDLE BROWSING PLAYER COSTS THE ALLOCATOR 12 REQUESTS AND 20916 BYTES PER MINUTE,
and ZERO while their tab is hidden or they are not on the BROWSE segment.

What that is not: it is not room capacity. This route reads the registry
and never touches a gameserver, so browsing cannot cost a live match a
frame and cannot fill a Machine. What a browser MIGHT do is make open
rooms findable enough that the 12-room ceiling binds sooner — which is
D5, is the developer's call, and is not answered here.

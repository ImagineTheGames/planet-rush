a0-67 — round two, the sixteen slots, measured
==============================================

Regenerate: npx vite-node evidence/a0-67-round-two/measure.ts <slot...>

Each block is one slot: the sound it SHIPS today, then the offers this round
puts on the board. The letters are the fresh ones — h/i/j/k on the fifteen
slots that were in the a0-60 sweep (a-c spent 2026-08-07, d-g spent
2026-08-17), g/h/i/j on oreCollect, which was never in that sweep.

An offer marked "(A/B)" is the incumbent itself, on the board under a letter
so that "keep what ships" is a verdict the review page can record.

What each column is for, and which sentence it answers:

  sec   length. oreCollect's "it shouldn't be too long"; the station death's
        1.32 s longest-tail invariant, which the three seconds of silence
        after it depend on (§4.7, §8).
  peak  the loudest sample. Every "more subtle" is a ceiling before it is a
        character — and round one broke it: three of four alarm offers and
        two of four thruster offers were LOUDER than the sound being
        complained about.
  rms   energy over the whole cue. The number that says whether a loop
        fatigues. thruster is held under half the shipped loop's.
  hf    share of energy above 3 kHz. "Annoying" lives up here (musicDread's
        denied take: 0.204) and so does "sparkle" (oreCollect) — the two
        complaints pull opposite ways, which is why they need one number
        each rather than a verdict about brightness.
  zc    zero crossings per sample past the attack: a cheap spectral centre,
        the same proxy audio.test.ts uses to keep rock and hull apart. It is
        what holds the §8 oreCollect/depositTick pair open.

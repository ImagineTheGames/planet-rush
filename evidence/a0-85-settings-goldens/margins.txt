==============================================================================

BEFORE = a0-77 as written (branch point ac7163c7, which predates a0-79).
AFTER  = the merged tree. Neither number is re-derived here: both are read
         off the shipped client (__mainMenu.settingsControls) by
         evidence/a0-77-settings-help/measure.spec.ts, re-run in the container.

a0-79 caps the settings GRID at MENU_COLUMN_SHARE of the band with a floor at
MENU_COLUMN_MIN. Where the cap already bit (desktop, at COLUMN.settings = 680)
or the floor wins (798x384), the ceiling is a NO-OP and a0-79 predicted so
itself — evidence/a0-79-menus/plate-room.txt, whose numbers these reproduce.

The two CI phone baselines — phone-landscape-settings and phone-portrait-settings
— are BOTH the 844x390 logical screen (the landscape one is the iphone profile
rotated to landscape; the portrait one is the same profile held portrait and
rotated by the lock). So the 10px each side above is what moved in both of them.

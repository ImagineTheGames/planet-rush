a0-16 — "the wave clock counts down to the wrong time"

A default match: DEFAULT_ABUNDANCE=scarce, so this world's waves
land every 165.0 s (WAVE_INTERVAL_S 150 × respawnInterval).
BEFORE = the HUD clock as it shipped, counting down the baseline 150 s.
AFTER  = the HUD clock now, counting down world.economy.waveInterval.
The gap between them is 15.0 s of dead air, below.

VERDICT: the shipped clock (BEFORE) emptied at t=150.00s and the rocks
         landed at t=165.00s — 15.0 s apart, every default match.
         The fixed clock (AFTER) runs out on the landing frame itself, and
         turns over to WAVE 2 on the same tick the sim does. It shows 0:01
         rather than 0:00 right up to the arrival because formatClock ceils —
         deliberately, so the HUD never reads 0:00 with a wave still pending.

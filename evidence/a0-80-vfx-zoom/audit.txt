0. THE REPORT
-------------

  "when the screen is 1.5x or 2 the thrusters draw In the wrong place"


1. THE CAUSE, AND WHY IT IS NOT ABOUT THRUSTERS
-----------------------------------------------
a0-74 zooms by scaling the renderer's world container:

    setCameraScale(scale) { …; this.worldRoot.scale.set(s); }

`worldRoot` holds ten layers — boundary, atmosphere, stations, turrets,
asteroids, chunks, muzzles, impacts, ships, shots (src/render/index.ts). The VFX
layer is not one of them. It was parented one level up, beside that container:

    gameRoot.addChild(vfxLayer);                         // src/main.ts:1529

...and chased the camera by writing its own position every frame from the
renderer's public projection of the world origin:

    renderer.projectToScreen(WORLD_ORIGIN, vfxOriginScratch);
    vfxLayer.x = vfxOriginScratch.x;
    vfxLayer.y = vfxOriginScratch.y;

That carries the camera's OFFSET and drops its SCALE. A particle at world point
P is therefore drawn at

    origin + P        instead of        origin + P·s

so the error is a pure function of where in the arena the emitter is:

    MISS = |P| · (1 − s),        s = cameraScale(step) = 1 / step

  rung    s      miss as a fraction of |P|
  ----    -----  -------------------------
  1x      1      0            ← the two spaces coincide: this is why it shipped
  1.5x    0.667  |P| / 3
  2x      0.5    |P| / 2

The arena is 2400 units across, so |P| for anything near the middle is ~1700 and
the miss is hundreds of CSS pixels on a 798-px-wide phone. THE THRUSTER IS NOT
SPECIAL. Every one of the 25 tells (art/tells.ts) goes through this one layer:
weapon impacts, ore collection, shield hits, spawn glows, the station-death
burst. The developer reported the one they happened to be watching.

3. THE FIX
----------
`Renderer.addWorldLayer(container)` — a public seam that adopts a caller-owned
container INTO `worldRoot`, appended, so it keeps exactly the depth it had (over
every entity layer, under the HUD the caller stacks above the renderer). main.ts
now says `renderer.addWorldLayer(vfxLayer)` and the per-frame position write is
deleted along with `WORLD_ORIGIN` and its scratch.

Multiplying particle coordinates by the camera scale at emission was rejected,
and the reason is the whole point of this bug: that leaves TWO owners of one
transform, and they disagree the first time either changes — which is precisely
what happened here the day a scale was added to a camera that only translated.
One container, one scale.

The seam is general rather than vfx-shaped on purpose. It is the answer to "how
does the next layer avoid being orphaned the same way": there is now a public way
to say "this is world-space" that carries the whole camera, instead of each new
layer re-deriving the parts of it that existed when its author wrote it.


4. THE REST OF THE CLASS — WHAT WAS CHECKED, NOT JUST WHAT WAS FIXED
---------------------------------------------------------------------
The brief asked for the class. Everything that rides `gameRoot` or `app.stage`
rather than `worldRoot` was walked:

  FIXED — a second crossing of the same seam, in the same shape:
    `projectToScreen` carries POINTS. Nothing carried LENGTHS. Three HUD feeds
    hand the layers an entity radius that the receiving contract declares in
    SCREEN pixels — `Combatant.radius` ("Entity radius in screen space, CSS px",
    the health bar hangs above by it), the nameplate's clearance
    (@ui/nameplates-view floats the label off `plate.radius`), and the Tap
    Commander lock reticle (its brackets sit just outside the target's screen
    radius) — and all three read `ship.radius` / `turret.radius` /
    `station.radius` straight off the sim, under a comment that said in as many
    words: "the camera renders 1:1, so a world radius is a screen radius". True
    until a0-74. At 2x it floated a bar a full hull-radius above a half-size
    ship. `Renderer.projectLength` is the length half of `projectToScreen`, off
    the same live transform; the three feeds go through it.
    VISIBLE IN THE PAIR: before-frozen-showcase-2x has the YOU nameplate a
    station-radius too high above its (half-size) station; after has it on the
    ring. Same in the 1.5x pair.

  CORRECT ALREADY — rides `projectToScreen`, so a0-74 carried it:
    nameplate + health-bar POSITIONS, tap waypoint / lock marker positions, the
    under-attack screen-edge arrow, the `?debug=1` centring hook. All read the
    renderer's ACTUAL worldRoot transform (a0-74's own comment says so) and were
    re-checked at every rung here.

  CORRECT ALREADY — screen space by design, nothing world-anchored in them:
    `hud` (all HUD chrome), `touchVisuals`, `fsAffordance`, `endOverlay`,
    `pauseView`, `pauseSettings`, and `badgeRoot` (build + ping badges, a
    deliberate stage sibling). None of these place anything relative to a world
    entity; they anchor to the content box.

  CORRECT ALREADY — the void backdrop (`app.stage`, behind worldRoot). It is
    screen-space parallax fed the camera OFFSET the renderer just wrote, and it
    anchors to no world object, so it has no emitter to miss. Its stars do not
    shrink with the rung; that is a look question for the Art Agent, not this
    defect, and the frames at all three rungs are here if the Director wants it
    raised.

  DELIBERATELY NOT SCALED — world-space records that must stay 1:1 with the sim:
    the tap hit-test candidate list (hit-tested in WORLD space after
    `logicalToWorld`, whose inverse already carries the scale), the pilot's own
    ship record, and every minimap datum (map space, not screen). Scaling any of
    these would be the same bug pointed the other way.


5. GOLDENS
----------
Not re-baselined, and this is a measurement rather than a hope:

  1. Every golden is taken at the default rung. `DEFAULT_VIEW_ZOOM` is
     VIEW_ZOOM_STEPS[0] = 1x, the control that changes it is touch-only, and no
     spec under tests/ calls `setZoom` / touches `viewZoom` (grepped).
  2. At 1x the camera scale is exactly 1, so both fixes are arithmetic no-ops:
     `P · 1 = P` and `projectLength(r) = r`.
  3. Proven, not argued: the frozen review sheet — the scene the goldens are
     taken on — is BYTE-IDENTICAL between the two builds at 1x (md5 above),
     nameplates, health bars, particles and all.

  Eyes on every image in ./frames and ./crops (18 + 12), before and after.


6. THE TEST
-----------
`effects ride the camera scale` — src/ui/zoom-control.test.ts. It lives with the
control rather than in the renderer's suite because the claim is the control's:
what this button does to the view, the effects do too. It drives the real
`Renderer` and the real `VfxField`/`VfxLayer` headless, walks every rung of
`VIEW_ZOOM_STEPS`, and asserts RELATIONSHIPS, never pixels (LESSONS §26):

  - a particle is drawn where the renderer projects its world point (Pixi's
    parent chain and the renderer's own projection, two routes to one number);
  - its screen gap from the emitter equals the world gap times `cameraScale`;
  - the whole plume stays within a ship-length SCALED of the hull;
  - `projectLength(r)` equals the gap between two world points r apart, projected.

Against the pre-fix wiring — reconstructed inside the spec, layer beside the
world container and offset from the camera read-back — it fails by 656 px at
1.5x and passes at 1x: ./test-on-broken-wiring.txt, with the variant used in
./pre-fix-wiring.reconstruction.ts.txt.

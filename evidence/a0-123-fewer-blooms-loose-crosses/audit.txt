4. THE CROSS IS ITS OWN DRAW — measured, not asserted.

   The rejected route was deriving the bit from a value already drawn, which
   costs no draw but correlates the cross with brightness or with colour. The
   stream this brief uses should show none. Pearson r over the bloomed stars,
   cross-bit against the star’s radius (monotone in magnitude) and against a
   hot/cool indicator (the whole of what temperature paints).

   mean r(cross, radius)      -0.00562   (sd across seeds 0.00926)
   mean r(cross, hot/cool)    0.00660   (sd across seeds 0.01570)
   1/sqrt(n) per seed         0.01468   <- what sd should be if independent

   Both means sit within one per-seed standard error of zero, and the spread
   across seeds is the spread independence predicts. Individual seeds reach
   2σ in both directions, which is what a coin does and what a DERIVED bit
   would not: taking the cross from the magnitude or the temperature pins r
   near a fixed non-zero value that does not average away with the seed.

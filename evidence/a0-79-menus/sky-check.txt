a0-79 — WHICH menu screens actually got the sky
======================================================================

Pixels that differ between `?sky=1` and `?sky=0` on the same screen, same
bundle. The goldens tolerate 1%, so anything under that would pass an old
baseline unchanged.

  desktop 1280x800     menu   21.00% differ  SKY IS THERE
  desktop 1280x800     doors  20.93% differ  SKY IS THERE
  phone 844x390 dpr3   menu   15.24% differ  SKY IS THERE
  phone 844x390 dpr3   doors  17.29% differ  SKY IS THERE

gate 2 — "colour comes from temperature, not magnitude"

  The assertion: DIFFERENT magnitude, SAME temperature ⇒ the SAME colour.
  `main` has no temperature at all — colour is a function of magnitude:

    mag 0.00   #7e8894
    mag 0.20   #7e8894
    mag 0.45   #a5acb4
    mag 0.80   #ffffff
    mag 0.86   #ffffff
    mag 1.00   #ffffff

    3 distinct colours over 6 magnitudes
    → assertion 2 FAILS: the same star at mag 0.20 and mag 0.80 paints
      #7e8894 and #ffffff.
    → assertion 3 FAILS: starRampColor.length = 1, and its one
      argument IS the magnitude, so the colour cannot help but ramp by it.

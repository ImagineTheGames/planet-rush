a0-12 — two maps with four stations a side. THE MIRROR, AS NUMBERS.
==============================================================================

The two sides are related by a POINT REFLECTION through the arena centre.
That isometry is chosen because `spawnHomeFields` stamps every home
neighbourhood by a ROTATION, so rotating the board by pi carries stations,
ships, home fields and the N-fold-symmetric commons onto themselves — side
equality is the same fact as "the stamp is a rotation", not a tuning result.

Read the left column top to bottom: that is the bug. It is the same sound as
the right column for four rows, and then it is a dead channel for a second and
a third while the mix around it is also at zero — three seconds of silence that
began mid-sting instead of after it. GDD §4.7 asks for the quiet to land ON TOP
OF the fall; the right column is a sound running out under a silence, which is
the picture the design describes ("nobody jokes for three seconds").

What the quiet still does (the other half of the claim)
------------------------------------------------------
  `mix` above is the multiplier every other voice gets, and it is 0.000 from
  0.12 s onward — the alarm, the ambience, the soundtrack, the UI cues, all of
  it. The exemption is one voice wide. `src/art/audio/engine.test.ts` asserts
  the same thing structurally (nothing else starts, and the four buses measure
  zero to the destination while the sting does not), and
  `src/art/vfx/death-moment.test.ts` pins the three numbers so a later pass
  cannot restore the old behaviour by moving the cut or trimming the quiet.
